Research & Innovation

Where Tradition Meets Technology

Gaurikrit’s research direction explores how a natural material understood by tradition can be developed with contemporary methods — into coatings, fuels, composites and building materials.

The Five Focus Areas Sustainability

Illustrative material study — an editorial visual, not a photograph of Gaurikrit products, a production line or an operational facility.

Focus Areas

Five directions under exploration

These are research directions — not proven accomplishments, patents or facilities. Each explores how a natural material could serve a contemporary application.

01

Natural Coatings

Wall coatings and finishes from naturally derived materials.

02

Biomass Energy

Fuel directions from natural biomass material streams.

03

Bio-Composites

Composite materials that carry natural fibres and minerals.

04

Carbon Reduction Technologies

Approaches that reduce reliance on fossil-based alternatives.

05

Sustainable Building Materials

Construction materials from renewable natural resources.

These focus areas represent research directions only. They are not presented as proven accomplishments, completed research, operational capabilities or commercially available products unless separately documented.

How we explore

Material-first, application-led

Four principles guide how Gaurikrit approaches each research direction.

01

Material-first

Each direction begins with a natural material and its properties, not a market template.

02

Application-led

Research is guided by real applications — walls, fuel, ritual and daily use.

03

Tradition-aware

Indian material traditions inform where technologies should serve, not erase.

04

Verified before published

Directions are shared as directions. Results are shared only once verified.
