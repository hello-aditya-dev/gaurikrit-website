गौरीकृत Gaurikrit Bio Products

One resource. One idea. A widening material story.

Gaurikrit Bio Products (OPC) Private Limited builds a material ecosystem around one natural resource — cow dung — from Prakritik Paint to fuel and utility directions. Based in Khurja, District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.

गौरीकृत Prakritik Paint
01 The Resource

A material long used in Indian homes.

Cow dung has long been used in traditional Indian wall and floor coatings — valued in daily rural life and treated as a useful material rather than a waste.

Gaurikrit begins from that recognition: a natural material already embedded in Indian material culture. Its Prakritik Paint carries that material idea into a contemporary paint format.

02 The Idea

Carry the resource into contemporary applications.

The idea is directional, not nostalgic: take a material India already understands and develop it into practical contemporary products — from naturally derived wall coatings to fuel and utility directions.

From naturally derived material to products for walls, energy and everyday use.

03 From Resource to Application

A journey of material stages — not dates.

Gaurikrit's story is told through what the material became, not when. The resource became a documented wall coating — and the same material now widens into fuel and utility directions.

Vision

A circular-economy company in the making.

To become a leading circular-economy company transforming natural and agricultural resources into sustainable products for homes, industries and communities.

Mission

Practical alternatives, responsibly made.

To develop practical, accessible and environmentally responsible material solutions — and to evaluate every direction for its environmental responsibility.
