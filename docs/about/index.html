Gaurikrit Bio Products (OPC) Private Limited is based in Khurja, District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.

01
Who we are

Legal identity.

Gaurikrit Bio Products (OPC) Private Limited is a private limited company registered in India. The studio is in Khurja, in District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.

A small, named organisation — not a faceless brand.

Gaurikrit works with bio-based materials. Prakritik Paint is the company's current paint presentation.

02
What we currently present

Two products, today.

Currently, Prakritik Paint is presented in two formats — Distemper and Emulsion. Both are white, matt, and suitable for interior and exterior walls.

Prakritik Distemper Paint
Eco-Friendly Cow Dung Paint · 1, 5, 10 & 20 kg · Matt · Interior & Exterior
Prakritik Emulsion Paint
Eco-Friendly Cow Dung Paint · 1, 4, 10 & 20 litre · Matt · Interior & Exterior
03
Our material direction

Cow dung, reconsidered.

Gaurikrit works with cow dung as a wall-coating material. The direction is material-first — what traditional Indian homes have long used, brought into a contemporary paint format.

प्रकृति से, दीवारों तक।

The wider context — agricultural-waste reuse, gaushala-led bio-products, rural material opportunity — is where the work sits, without quantifying it.

04
Mission

A simple statement.

Transforming waste into wonder, one wall at a time.

One wall at a time. The work is patient — gathered, prepared, blended, applied.

05 · Brand principle
प्रकृति से, दीवारों तक

Good for Nature. Good for Life.

The principle is short, on purpose. The work is to bring a familiar material into a contemporary paint format — and to keep that material honest.
