गौरीकृत Gaurikrit Bio Products

Nature. Culture. Useful materials.

Gaurikrit Bio Products (OPC) Private Limited makes Prakritik Paint — a cow dung-based paint in two formats, Distemper and Emulsion, for interior and exterior walls. From Khurja, District Bulandshahr.

Who we are

Gaurikrit Bio Products (OPC) Private Limited — a One Person Company registered in District Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh – 203131.

Gaurikrit makes Prakritik Paint, a cow dung-based paint in two formats: Prakritik Distemper (powder) and Prakritik Emulsion (liquid). Both are matt finish, listed for interior and exterior use.

The company carries an old Indian material idea — cow dung on walls — into a contemporary paint format. Not nostalgia. Not novelty. A useful material, reconsidered for modern walls.

Material direction

An old Indian material idea, carried into a contemporary paint format.

Cow dung has been used on Indian walls and floors for generations. Prakritik Paint takes that material and processes it into a workable binder, blended into a modern paint.

The cow is in the material. The wall is where it goes.
