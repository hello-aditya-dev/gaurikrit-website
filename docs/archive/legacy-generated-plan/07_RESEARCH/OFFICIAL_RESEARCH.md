# Official Research

Sources consulted to frame the Gaurikrit brand and category truthfully.
All marketing claims in `claims-register.json` cite one of these.

## Turmeric (Haldi)
- **Curcumin content** of Lakadong turmeric can reach 6–7%; common Indian varieties 2–4%. Reference: IBG News / Lakadong-specific reports (2023).
- **Anti-inflammatory & antioxidant** properties of curcumin are widely cited in peer-reviewed literature; bioavailability improves with piperine.
- **FSSAI** regulates turmeric as a spice and food additive in India.
- **Traditional use** of haldi in Indian households spans 4,000+ years (Ayurvedic texts).

## Paint (Indian market)
- **Low-VOC** paints are defined under BIS / GreenPro norms; VOC < 50 g/L for interior emulsions is a common premium benchmark.
- **Coverage** for interior emulsion: ~140–160 sq ft per litre per coat (industry norm).
- **Weatherability** for premium exterior paints: 7–8 years is a typical marketed warranty.
- **GreenPro** certification (CII-IGBC) is India's recognised ecolabel for paints.

## Brand identity references
- "Black & White" and "Haldi & Black" PDFs supplied by client define the colour discipline.
- The turmeric-gold + charcoal palette is derived from those.
