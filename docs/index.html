The material idea
प्रकृति से

An old Indian material idea, reconsidered for modern walls.

Traditional Indian homes have long used cow-dung-based wall coatings. Prakritik Paint brings that material idea into a contemporary paint format.

The result is a wall coating that carries a familiar material lineage — re-expressed as a workable modern paint.

Mission
प्रकृति से, दीवारों तक

Transforming waste into wonder, one wall at a time.

Gaurikrit Bio Products works with a familiar Indian material — cow dung — and brings it into a contemporary paint format. The work sits at the intersection of agricultural reuse, rural opportunity, and eco-friendly wall coatings.
