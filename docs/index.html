The Gaurikrit Story

From Waste to Resource.

Traditional India has long recognised cow dung as a useful material. Gaurikrit's direction is to carry that resource into practical contemporary applications — from naturally derived material to products for walls, energy and everyday use.

Stage 01

Raw Material

Cow dung — a natural material long valued in traditional Indian homes.

Stage 02

Material Development

The resource is processed and developed into useful working materials.

Stage 03

Useful Applications

Products for walls, energy and everyday use — nothing goes to waste.

The Circular Model

Nothing Goes to Waste.

One continuous cycle: the resource enters, is developed, becomes useful products — and creates a new value cycle.

  1. 01

    Collection

    Natural and agricultural material streams are gathered.

  2. 02

    Processing

    The raw material is prepared and stabilised.

  3. 03

    Material Enhancement

    It is developed into useful working materials.

  4. 04

    Manufacturing

    Materials are formed into product families.

  5. 05

    Products

    Walls, energy and everyday-use applications.

  6. 06

    Environmental Impact

    Each use replaces a less sustainable alternative.

  7. 07

    Resource Regeneration

    The cycle renews — nothing goes to waste.
