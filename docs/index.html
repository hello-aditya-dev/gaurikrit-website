The Gaurikrit Story

From Waste to Resource.

Traditional India has long recognised cow dung as a useful material. Gaurikrit's direction is to carry that resource into practical contemporary applications — from naturally derived material to products for walls, energy and everyday use.

Our Mission

Transforming Cow Dung into Innovative Solutions for Sustainable Living.

Stage 01

Cow Dung

A natural material long valued in traditional Indian homes — the shared starting point for every Gaurikrit direction.

Stage 02

Processing & Innovation

The resource is studied, processed and developed into useful working materials — from coatings to fuel formats.

Stage 03

Product Portfolio

Four solutions from one resource: Eco-Paints, Cow-Dung Logs, Bio-Briquettes and Eco Utility Products.

Stage 04

Environmental Impact

Products that carry natural material back into everyday use — building a circular-economy direction for the future.

The Gaurikrit Ecosystem

One resource. Four solutions.

Cow dung is the shared material beginning. Gaurikrit develops it in four directions — Eco-Paints is the documented family; Cow-Dung Logs is a development direction; Bio-Briquettes and Eco Utility Products are Futureline product directions, not yet in production.

Every product we create contributes to a circular economy where waste becomes value and sustainability becomes practical.

01

Eco-Paints

Healthy walls inspired by nature.

Cow dung-based wall coatings in Distemper and Emulsion formats — the most developed family in the Gaurikrit ecosystem, documented with full product specifications.

Documented family

  • Eco-conscious formulation
  • Sustainable building applications
  • Natural-finish positioning

Cow-Dung Logs

Saving trees without changing traditions.
