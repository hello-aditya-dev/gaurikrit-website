The Gaurikrit Story

From Waste to Resource.

Traditional India has long recognised cow dung as a useful material. Gaurikrit's direction is to carry that resource into practical contemporary applications — from naturally derived material to products for walls, energy and everyday use.

Stage 01

Raw Material

Cow dung — a natural material long valued in traditional Indian homes.

Stage 02

Material Development

The resource is processed and developed into useful working materials.

Stage 03

Useful Applications

Products and material directions for walls, energy and everyday use.

The Circular Model

A Circular Material Direction.

One continuous cycle: the resource enters, is developed, becomes useful products — and the material cycle continues.

  1. 01

    Resource

    Natural and agricultural material streams.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    Material is prepared for further development.

  3. 03

    Material Development

    Material is developed into useful formats.

  4. 04

    Production

    Documented or developing applications take shape.

  5. 05

    Application

    Products and material directions are put to practical use.

  6. 06

    Review

    Use, material value and environmental considerations are evaluated.

  7. 07

    Next Cycle

    Further reuse and material opportunities are explored.
