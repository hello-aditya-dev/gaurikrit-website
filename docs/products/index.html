Prakritik Paint

Cow dung-based paint in Distemper and Emulsion formats.

Two Prakritik Paint formats — both matt, both suitable for interior and exterior walls, both carrying the same material lineage.

01 · Distemper
Eco-Friendly Cow Dung Paint

Prakritik Distemper Paint

A powdered cow dung-based paint format. Packaged in kilograms, suitable for interior and exterior walls, with a matt finish.

1, 5, 10 & 20 kg Finish: Matt Interior & Exterior Coverage: 200 sq.ft.**

02 · Emulsion
Eco-Friendly Cow Dung Paint

Prakritik Emulsion Paint

A liquid cow dung-based paint format. Packaged in litres, suitable for interior and exterior walls, with a matt finish.

1, 4, 10 & 20 litre Finish: Matt Interior & Exterior Coverage: 300 sq.ft.**
