Why Prakritik

An old material idea, reconsidered for modern walls.

A short reading of where Prakritik Paint comes from — the material, the tradition it carries, and the contemporary paint format it has become.

01
The material

Cow dung, as a wall-coating material.

Cow dung is a familiar material in Indian domestic life. It has long been used as a wall and floor coating — a way of refreshing a surface and bringing an everyday material back into the home.

The material idea is older than the paint format.

Prakritik Paint begins with this material — gathered, prepared, and brought into a paint format that can be applied with a brush.

02
The tradition

A traditional Indian wall practice.

Traditional Indian homes have long used cow-dung-based wall coatings. The practice is domestic — part of cleaning and refreshing living spaces — and rural, tied to the rhythms of the household and the herd.

Prakritik Paint does not replace the practice. It carries the material idea into a contemporary paint format that can be applied to interior and exterior walls.

03
From material to paint

Five stages, from nature to the wall.

The journey from cow dung to painted wall passes through five stages — gathered, prepared, blended, applied.

  1. 01
    Natural origin
    The material begins with the cow.
  2. 02
    Raw material
    Cow dung, gathered and prepared.
  3. 03
    Preparation
    Processed into a workable binder.
  4. 04
    Prakritik Paint
    Blended into a contemporary paint format.
  5. 05
    Finished wall
    Applied to interior and exterior walls.

06
Sustainability context

Where the work sits.

Prakritik Paint sits at the intersection of a few threads — without quantifying any of them.

  • Agricultural-waste reuse. Cow dung is repurposed into a wall-coating material.
  • Gaushala context. A natural fit with gaushala-led bio-product conversations.
  • Rural opportunity. The material and the work sit close to rural India.
  • Eco-friendly framing. A wall coating read alongside the wider eco-friendly conversation.

Material reuse, not a quantified claim.
