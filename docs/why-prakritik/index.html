An old Indian material idea

An old material idea, reconsidered for modern walls.

Traditional Indian homes have long used cow-dung-based coatings on walls and floors. Prakritik Paint brings that material idea into a contemporary paint format.

01 The material

A natural material for modern walls.

Cow dung has a long history of use on walls and floors in India.

Gaurikrit offers cow dung-based Prakritik Paint in Distemper and Emulsion formats.

Not a novelty. A useful material, reconsidered.

02 The tradition

Limewashed walls, courtyard elevations.

Indian vernacular architecture is full of limewashed walls, plinths, verandahs, and rectangular openings — plaster, lime and earth.

The courtyard study shows a wall surface in an everyday Indian setting.

03 Material to wall

From a natural material to a finished wall.

The raw material, the paint made from it, and the finished surface — three steps, one material idea.
